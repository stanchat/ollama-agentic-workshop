CommunityInsight.AI helps cities, school districts, nonprofits, and coalitions find and track grants faster.
Typical steps:
1) Define topic filters (education, housing, health).
2) Use federal sources (Grants.gov Search2), add state/local portals.
3) Save candidates to watchlist; set alerts for updates.
4) Track deadlines, eligibility, and award ranges.
5) Share candidates with your team and record notes/outcomes.
